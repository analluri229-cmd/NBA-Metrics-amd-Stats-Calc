# NBA Player Evaluation Metrics Guide

This document provides comprehensive explanations of the key basketball analytics metrics used to evaluate player performance in the NBA.

---

## Efficiency Metrics

### Player Efficiency Rating (PER)
A comprehensive metric that measures a player's per-minute productivity. PER adjusts for league pace and accounts for both offensive and defensive contributions. A PER of 15.0 is considered average for the league.

### Adjusted Player Efficiency Rating (APER)
An improved version of PER that adjusts for team context and other factors. APER provides a more accurate assessment of a player's true impact by removing team-specific biases.

### True Shooting Percentage (TS%)
Measures the efficiency of a player's shooting by accounting for field goals, three-pointers, and free throws. The formula is: `Points / (2 × True Shooting Attempts)`. This metric is more comprehensive than traditional FG% because it accounts for the different point values of shots.

### Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)
Adjusts field goal percentage to account for the fact that three-pointers are worth more than two-pointers. Formula: `(FG + 0.5 × 3P) / FGA`. A useful metric for evaluating overall shooting efficiency.

### Field Goal Percentage (FG%)
The basic shooting percentage: `Made Field Goals / Field Goal Attempts`. Includes both two-pointers and three-pointers.

### Three-Point Percentage (3P%)
Measures the accuracy of three-point shooting: `Made Three-Pointers / Three-Point Attempts`.

### Free Throw Percentage (FT%)
Measures the accuracy of free throw shooting: `Made Free Throws / Free Throw Attempts`.

## Plus-Minus Variants

### Real Plus-Minus (RPM)
Estimates how much a player affects their team's point differential per 100 possessions. RPM uses regularized regression to isolate individual player impact. Positive values indicate the team scores more when the player is on court.

### Adjusted Plus-Minus (APM)
A regression-based metric that estimates a player's impact on team point differential. It accounts for teammates and opponents to isolate individual contribution. Values are per 100 possessions.

### Box Plus-Minus (BPM)
Calculates a player's impact on team performance using box score statistics without needing lineup data. BPM estimates how many points per 100 possessions a player contributes relative to a league-average replacement player.

### Defensive Plus-Minus (DPM)
Isolates a player's defensive impact on point differential. A positive DPM indicates the opposing team scores fewer points when the player is on the court.

### Net Plus-Minus (NetPM)
The overall plus-minus statistic showing the net point differential when a player is on the court: `Points Scored While Player On Court - Points Allowed While Player On Court`.

---

## Advanced Composites

### LEBRON
An advanced metric developed by ESPN that estimates a player's total impact (offense, defense, rebounding, and spacing). LEBRON stands for "Luck-Adjusted Estimated Plus-Minus (Regressed)" and produces a Wins Above Replacement Player estimate.

### RAPTOR (Regularized Adjustment Plus-Minus Opponent Tracking On/Off Replacement)
A metric from FiveThirtyEight that measures player impact using tracking data and on/off analysis. It breaks down impact into offensive and defensive components.

### CARMELO (Catches and Releases Made Estimation and League Offensive)
A forward-looking projection metric from FiveThirtyEight that estimates future player performance based on historical trajectory and comparable players.

### DARKO (Defense-Adjusted Rating Konveyor Optimized)
An advanced metric that specifically focuses on isolating defensive impact from overall performance using comprehensive statistical methods.

---

## Value Metrics

### Wins Above Replacement Player (WARP)
Estimates how many additional wins a player provides compared to a replacement-level player (typically a minimum-salary player). A WARP of 5.0 indicates a star-level player.

### Win Shares (WS/48)
Estimates the portion of wins generated by a player per 48 minutes (full game). It allocates team wins among players based on their statistical contributions. A league-average player averages around 0.100 WS/48.

### Approximate Value (AV)
A simplified value metric that estimates overall player contribution. It's calculated from basic box score statistics and represents a ballpark figure of wins contributed.

### Box Score Plus/Minus (See Box Plus-Minus above)
Also referred to as BPM; measures impact using only box score data.

---

## Usage & Possessions Metrics

### Usage Rate
Measures the percentage of team possessions a player uses while on the court. Formula: `(FGA + 0.44 × FTA + TOV) / Team Possessions × 100`. Higher usage rates indicate a player is more involved in offensive plays.

### Touches
The total number of times a player receives the ball during a game or season.

### Total Player Possessions
The number of possessions in which a player is actively involved (touches, free throw attempts, etc.).

### Scoring Possessions
Possessions in which a player scores or assists on a basket.

### Non-Scoring Possessions
Possessions in which a player touches the ball but does not score or assist on a score.

### Turnover Rate (TO%)
The percentage of possessions that end in a turnover: `TOV / Total Possessions × 100`.

---

## Fantasy & Game Metrics

### DraftKings Fantasy Points
Fantasy points awarded in DraftKings contests based on actual game statistics. Points vary by player position and include bonuses for thresholds (e.g., triple-doubles).

### FanDuel Fantasy Points
Fantasy points awarded in FanDuel contests based on a different scoring system than DraftKings. Scoring allocations differ by position.

### Game Score
A simple metric that estimates a player's overall contribution in a single game by combining points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, and turnovers with specific weightings.

### Win Probability Added (WPA)
Measures how much a player's actions during a game changed their team's probability of winning. Positive values indicate clutch performances that increased win probability.

---

## Rebounding Metrics

### Rebound Rate (REB%)
The percentage of available rebounds a player grabbed while on the court: `Rebounds / (Rebounds Available While On Court)`.

### Offensive Rebound Rate (ORB%)
The percentage of available offensive rebounds grabbed: `Offensive Rebounds / (Offensive Rebounds Available While On Court)`.

### Defensive Rebound Rate (DRB%)
The percentage of available defensive rebounds grabbed: `Defensive Rebounds / (Defensive Rebounds Available While On Court)`.

---

## Assists & Ball Movement Metrics

### Assist Ratio
The percentage of a player's possessions that result in assists: `Assists / Possessions`.

### Assist-to-Turnover Ratio (A/TO)
Measures playmaking efficiency by comparing assists to turnovers. Higher ratios indicate better ball handling and decision-making.

### True Assist (TA)
Adjusts for the quality of assists, giving credit for passes that lead to assisted baskets, weighted by shooting difficulty and distance.

### Secondary Assist
An assist that creates a situation for an assisted basket (two passes before the made shot).

---

## Defensive Metrics

### Steals Per Game (SPG)
The average number of steals a player records per game.

### Steal Rate (STL%)
The percentage of opponent possessions that end in a steal while the player is on defense: `Steals / Opponent Possessions × 100`.

### Blocks Per Game (BPG)
The average number of shots a player blocks per game.

### Block Rate (BLK%)
The percentage of opponent two-pointers a player blocks while on defense.

### Defensive Rating (DRtg)
Points allowed per 100 defensive possessions. Lower values indicate better defense. `(Opponent Points / Opponent Possessions) × 100`.

### Defensive Box Plus/Minus
The defensive component of Box Plus-Minus, isolating defensive impact.

---

## Spacing & Pace Metrics

### Pace Factor
Measures the tempo of play - how many possessions per game. `(Possessions / (Game Minutes / 48))`. Higher pace means more fast-paced games.

### Offensive Rating (ORtg)
Points produced per 100 offensive possessions. `(Points × 100) / Possessions`. Higher values indicate more efficient offense.

### Spacing Metrics
Advanced metrics that measure how a player's shooting ability affects spacing and creates opportunities for teammates.

---

## Shooting Distribution Metrics

### Assisted Field Goal Percentage (AstFG%)
The percentage of a player's made field goals that were assisted: `Assisted FG / Total FG × 100`.

### True Shot Attempts (TSA)
Accounts for all shooting: `FGA + 0.44 × FTA`.

### High-Value Shot Percentage
The percentage of a player's shots that come from three-point range or the paint.

---

## Advanced Adjusted Metrics

### Adjusted Offensive Rating (AdjORtg)
Offensive rating adjusted for opponent strength and pace.

### Adjusted Defensive Rating (AdjDRtg)
Defensive rating adjusted for opponent strength and pace.

### Four Factors
Analyzes team/player performance based on:
1. **Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)**
2. **Turnover Rate (TO%)**
3. **Offensive Rebound Rate (ORB%)**
4. **Free Throw Rate (FTA/FGA)**

---

## Summary: How to Use These Metrics

- **For Overall Value**: WARP, BPM, or RAPTOR provide comprehensive impact assessments
- **For Efficiency**: PER, TS%, and eFG% show how well a player scores
- **For Volume**: Usage Rate and Touches show how involved a player is
- **For Defense**: Defensive Rating and DPM isolate defensive contribution
- **For Advanced Analysis**: Combine multiple metrics for a complete picture rather than relying on a single statistic

No single metric tells the complete story - use these in combination with watching actual game film for comprehensive player evaluation.
